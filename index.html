Selected Projects

A look into systems and platforms I've built. For detailed walkthroughs, visit the case studies.

SmartNoteZ

AI study & quiz platform

Upload PDF, TXT or Markdown notes and get an AI-generated timed quiz. Text is extracted in the browser with PDF.js, quizzes come from Gemini/Groq as JSON, and results are charted with Chart.js. Live demo available.

Node.jsSupabaseGemini / GroqChart.js

Avaia

AI voice assistant & smart dustbin

A voice-controlled assistant that turns natural-language commands into actions with Whisper and OpenAI LLMs. A Flask backend sends HTTP commands to an ESP32 that drives a robotic dustbin. Presented at a Computer Science exhibition.

PythonFlaskWhisperESP32

GuideX

Automatic blind stick

An assistive stick for visually impaired users that detects obstacles with an ultrasonic sensor and alerts the user, built on an Arduino Nano.
