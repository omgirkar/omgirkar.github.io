Building This Portfolio

SEO Strategy Web Development Backlink Building Performance

Overview

This case study documents the process of building my personal developer portfolio from the ground up—with a primary focus on search engine visibility and strategic link acquisition. The goal was not only to showcase my work but to create a discoverable asset that drives organic traffic, builds domain authority, and serves as a central hub for my professional online presence.

From technical SEO foundations to a manual backlink outreach campaign, every decision was made to maximise the site's ranking potential while maintaining a clean, performant, and user-friendly experience.

Technical SEO & Performance

  • Semantic HTML & Structured Data: Used proper heading hierarchy, `
    ` and `
    ` tags, and implemented JSON‑LD (Person schema) to help search engines understand the content and author.
  • Meta Tags & Open Graph: Every page has unique, keyword‑rich titles, descriptions, and OG tags for better click‑through rates on social platforms.
  • Page Speed & Core Web Vitals: Achieved 90+ on Lighthouse by using lazy loading, minified assets, preconnecting to fonts, and serving images in modern formats (WebP).
  • Sitemap & Robots.txt: Created an XML sitemap and a well‑configured robots.txt to guide crawlers efficiently.
  • Canonical URLs & HTTPS: Used canonical tags to avoid duplicate content and enforced HTTPS with HSTS for security and ranking boost.

Content & Keyword Strategy

  • Target Keywords: Researched and targeted terms like “developer portfolio Mumbai”, “full‑stack developer case studies”, and “AI IoT projects” to attract both local and niche traffic.
  • Rich Case Studies: Each project page (like this one) is written in depth, covering architecture, decisions, and results—providing value to readers and demonstrating expertise.
  • Internal Linking: Strategically linked between projects and the homepage to distribute link equity and help crawlers discover all pages.

Backlink Acquisition Strategy

Building quality backlinks was a deliberate, multi‑channel effort. Here’s exactly what I did:

  • Guest Blogging: Wrote technical tutorials and thought‑leadership articles for developer blogs and industry sites, including a do‑follow link back to my portfolio. (e.g., a post on “Building an AI Voice Assistant with ESP32” on a popular IoT blog.)
  • Open Source Contributions: Added my portfolio URL to my GitHub profile, pinned repositories, and README files of projects I contribute to—earning links from high‑authority developer platforms.
  • Community Engagement: Shared my case studies on LinkedIn, Reddit (r/webdev, r/SEO), and Dev.to, where they gained organic traction and attracted natural backlinks from other developers.
  • Direct Outreach: Personally reached out to 15+ industry peers, mentors, and local tech communities, asking for feedback and encouraging them to link to relevant project pages.
  • Broken Link Building: Identified broken resource links on other portfolios and offered my content as a replacement—resulting in 3 high‑value backlinks.

Within two months, I acquired over 15 backlinks from domains with DR 40+, significantly boosting the site’s authority and ranking.

Results & Impact

  • Search Rankings: Achieved 273+ search impressions and 12 organic clicks during the initial growth phase, with appearances for branded and project-related searches such as "Girkar", "GuideX", and "Avaia Logic".
  • Google Search Console Integration: Successfully indexed the portfolio with Google Search Console, enabling monitoring of search performance, indexing status, and keyword visibility.
  • Technical SEO: Built a strong SEO foundation by implementing semantic HTML5, structured data (JSON-LD), XML sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, optimized metadata, Open Graph tags, and responsive design.
  • Personal Branding: Created an SEO-focused portfolio that showcases my expertise in web development, SEO, AI, and IoT while serving as a practical demonstration of real-world technical SEO implementation.

This project proved that a well‑crafted portfolio, backed by solid SEO and proactive link building, can be a powerful career asset.
